AYRA RUNWAY ATELIER — bilingual fashion site
- English / Kiswahili toggle in header.
- Real runway video clips stream from Pexels. Internet is required for these video clips.
- The Pexels video clips are listed as free under the Pexels License; check the individual clip and current license before commercial publishing.

- Open index.html in Chrome or upload the full folder to Netlify/GitHub Pages. Keep images/ and audio/ folders.
